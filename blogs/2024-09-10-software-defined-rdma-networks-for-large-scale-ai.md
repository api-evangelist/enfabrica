Photo by Christopher Burns on Unsplash The Bifurcation of SDN and RDMA Networks SDN (Software Defined Networking) evolved in the last decade and became the de facto standard as it made networking suitable for cloud scale, delivering significant operational efficiencies. SDN gave data center operators two fundamental advantages related to control and flexibility. Over the last decade, data center operators improved operational metrics such as faster and predictive roll out of new features and capabilities.
